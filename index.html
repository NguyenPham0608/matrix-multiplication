Sizes come from sysctl on an M5 (performance-core values). Matrices are 8-byte doubles, stored row by row, each starting on a fresh line. Because sets are picked by address, power-of-two row lengths like 512 make a column of B land in just a few sets, which causes conflict misses that the fully associative teaching cache can't show.
